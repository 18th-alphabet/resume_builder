    Format notes
    • PDF prints the preview. Use 100% scale with browser headers off.
    • HTML retains markup and CSS; longer text may reflow.
    • Native DOCX retains its package. Browser and Word layouts may differ.
    • Converted DOCX uses embedded HTML; open and save in Word before portal upload.
    • DOC is Word-compatible HTML. TXT and converted RTF have limited formatting.
    • PDF scans need OCR; binary DOC recovery is best-effort. ODT keeps its source package.
    • Check the destination portal’s accepted formats.
